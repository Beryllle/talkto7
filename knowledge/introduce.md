深圳国家高技术产业创新中心

（深圳发展改革研究院）单位简介

（深圳国家高技术产业创新中心提供）

深圳国家高技术产业创新中心（以下简称"深国创中心"），在深圳，成立于1992年，由国家发展改革委和深圳市政府联合组建，加挂"深圳发展改革研究院"牌子，非营利性公共技术服务机构，企业化管理事业单位，法定机构试点，归口深圳市发展改革委管理。内设智能经济研究所、生物经济所、新材料产业所、海洋经济所、能源经济所、绿色发展研究所、轨道交通与城市发展所、服务业与社会民生所、经济运行所（投资研究所）、改革创新所、区域发展所、重大科技基础设施部（低空经济所）、创新发展部、大数据平台与信息部等业务部门。现有员工近300人，其中，研究员硕士及以上学历占比100%、985/211院校毕业占比近60%、QS50院校毕业占比近40%。

成立三十三年来，深国创中心立足发展战略、创新政策、产业规划、区域规划、平台策划、项目评审等六大核心业务，**着力打造中国特色新型智库平台，**支撑国家部委开展重大战略问题研究近20项，提升深圳市制造业竞争力研究、粤港澳大湾区要素便捷高效流动问题研究分获国家发展改革委优秀研究成果二等及三等奖；主动承担党委政府重大战略、重大问题研究任务超500项，报送中办国办省委市委党政信息超300篇；全程参与河套深港科技创新合作区、光明科学城等国家重大平台谋划建设，深度参与国家级、市级重大规划编制以及综合改革试点等重大改革纵深推进。**全领域支撑深圳现代化产业体系建设，**全面贯彻落实市委市政府关于发展壮大战略性新兴产业、加快培育未来产业工作部署要求，实现"20+8"产业集群研究领域全覆盖，扶持政策、项目招引、平台谋划、成果转化全支撑，连续四年编制《深圳市"20+8"产业研究报告》，组织开展重大产业项目、重大创新平台等项目评审超6000项，储备专家库超6000名、企业库超5000家。**以服务链牵引"四链融合"打造大湾区科技产业协同创新引领者，**充分发挥"懂产业+懂政策+懂平台+懂项目"的综合优势，依托粤港澳大湾区大数据中心、粤港澳大湾区工业互联网公共技术服务平台、国际创新产业信息服务平台，探索以智库咨询服务链串联牵引产业链、创新链、资金链、人才链加速渗透融合，显著提升创新体系整体效能。

**State High-Tech Industrial Innovation Center，Shenzhen**

**(Shenzhen Development and Reform Research Institute)**

State High-Tech Industrial Innovation Center，Shenzhen (hereinafter referred to as HIIC), founded in 1992, is jointly established by the National Development and Reform Commission (NDRC) and Shenzhen Municipal People's Government. It also operates under the name of Shenzhen Development and Reform Research Institute. As a non-profit public technical service institution, a public institution under enterprise-style management and a pilot statutory body, it is affiliated to and managed by Development & Reform Commission of Shenzhen Municipal Government.

HIIC houses a full set of professional research and business departments, including:

Institute of Digital Economy, Institute of Bio-Economy, Institute of New Materials Industry, Institute of Marine Economy, Institute of Energy Economy, Institute of Green Economy, Institute of Rail Transit and Urban Development, Institute of Service Industry and Social Livelihood, Institute of Economic Operation, Institute of Reform and Innovation, Institute of Regional Development, Major Science & Technology Infrastructure Department (Institute of Low-Altitude Economy), Innovation Development Department, and Big Data Platform & Information Department.

The center employs nearly 300 staff members. Among its researchers:

- 100% hold a master's degree or above;
- Nearly 60% graduated from Project 985/211 universities;
- Nearly 40% graduated from universities ranked in the global QS Top 50.

Over the 33 years since its establishment, HIIC has focused on six core businesses:

development strategy, innovation policy, industrial planning, regional planning, platform design, and project evaluation. It is committed to building a new-type think tank with Chinese characteristics.

It has supported national ministries and commissions in nearly 20 major strategic research projects. Its research Enhancing the Competitiveness of Shenzhen's Manufacturing Industry won the Second Prize and Research on the Convenient and Efficient Flow of Factors in the Guangdong-Hong Kong-Macao Greater Bay Area won the Third Prize for Excellent Research Achievements awarded by the NDRC.

It has proactively undertaken more than 500 major strategic and policy research tasks for Party committees and governments at all levels, and submitted over 300 policy reports to the General Offices of the CPC Central Committee, the State Council, as well as provincial and municipal authorities.

It has fully participated in the planning and construction of major national platforms such as the Hetao Shenzhen-Hong Kong Science and Technology Innovation Cooperation Zone and Guangming Science City, and deeply engaged in the formulation of key national and municipal plans and the in-depth advancement of major reforms including the comprehensive reform pilot program.

HIIC provides full-domain support for the development of Shenzhen's modern industrial system, fully implementing the municipal government's strategy to expand strategic emerging industries and accelerate future industries. It covers the entire research spectrum of Shenzhen's"20+8"industrial clusters, offering one-stop support in policy design, project attraction, platform development and achievement commercialization.

It has published the Shenzhen "20+8" Industrial Research Report for four consecutive years. Meanwhile, it has organized reviews for more than 6,000 major industrial and innovation platform projects, with an expert pool of over 6,000 experts and an enterprise database of over 5,000 enterprises.

Guided by the service chain to drive the integration of four chains (industrial, innovation, capital and talent chains), HIIC aims to become a leader in sci-tech and industrial collaborative innovation in the Greater Bay Area.

Relying on its unique strengths in industry, policy, platform and project expertise, and supported by the Guangdong-Hong Kong-Macao Greater Bay Area Big Data Center, the Greater Bay Area Industrial Internet Public Technical Service Platform, and the International Innovation & Industry Information Service Platform, it promotes the deep integration of industrial, innovation, capital and talent chains via think tank and consulting services, significantly boosting the overall efficiency of the innovation ecosystem.
